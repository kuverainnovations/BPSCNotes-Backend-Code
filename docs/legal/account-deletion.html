Delete your BPSCNotes account

You can permanently delete your BPSCNotes account and the data attached to it. There are two ways to do it.

Option 1 — In the app (fastest)

  1. Open BPSCNotes and go to Settings.
  2. Scroll to Delete Account.
  3. Confirm. Your account is deleted immediately and you are signed out.

Option 2 — Request it here

If you can no longer sign in, email support@bpscnotes.in with the subject "Account deletion request" and include the mobile number registered to the account.

We verify that the request came from the account holder before deleting anything, then complete the deletion within 7 days and confirm by email.

What gets deleted

  • Your profile — name, photo, mobile number, district and exam preferences
  • Your study history — quiz attempts, scores, streaks and study time
  • Your coins balance and reward history
  • Your notebook notes and written answers
  • Study material you uploaded to the marketplace
  • Your chat messages and peer reviews
  • Your notification tokens and app settings

What we keep, and for how long

  • Purchase and invoice records — up to 8 years. Indian tax law requires us to retain these. They are kept separately from your profile and are not used for anything else.
  • Moderation records — up to 2 years. If your account was reported or suspended, we keep that record so the same abuse can be recognised if it recurs.
  • Anonymised analytics. Aggregated usage statistics that can no longer be linked to you.

Before you delete

Deletion cannot be undone. Any coins, purchased courses, study material and active subscription access are lost and are not refundable on deletion. If you have an active subscription bought through Google Play, cancel it in the Play Store separately — deleting your BPSCNotes account does not cancel a Play subscription, and Google will keep billing it otherwise.

Questions
