Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 August 2026

This policy explains what BPSCNotes ("we", "the app") collects, why, who we share it with, and what you can do about it. It covers the BPSCNotes Android app and the services at bpscnotes.in. BPSCNotes is operated by [[LEGAL_ENTITY]], [[REGISTERED_ADDRESS]].

1. What we collect

Information you give us

  • Mobile number. Used to create your account and to sign you in with a one-time password. This is the only identifier we require.
  • Profile details. Your name, and optionally a profile photo, district, target exam and preparation level. Used to personalise study content and to show your name on leaderboards and in study rooms.
  • Content you create. Written answers, peer reviews you leave on other students' answers, notebook notes, chat messages in study rooms, and study material you upload to the marketplace. Notebook notes are private to you. The rest is visible to other users, as described in each feature.
  • Reports you submit. If you report content or block another user, we store what you reported and why, so our moderation team can act on it.

Information collected automatically

  • Advertising ID. The app shows ads through Google AdMob, which uses your device's advertising ID to select and measure ads and to limit how often you see the same one. You can reset or delete this ID at any time in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads.
  • Usage analytics. Which screens you open, which features you use, quiz and study activity, crashes and errors. Used to fix problems and decide what to build. Collected through Firebase Analytics and PostHog.
  • Device information. Device model, Android version, app version, language and approximate region. Used for compatibility and support.
  • Study activity. Time studied, streaks, quiz scores, coins earned and spent. This drives your progress screens, leaderboards and rewards.

Device permissions we ask for

Each of these is requested only when you use the feature that needs it, and the app remains usable if you decline.

  • Camera. To photograph a handwritten answer when you submit one, and to appear on video if you join a live class. Photos you attach to an answer are uploaded with that answer. Video in a live class is streamed to that class and is not recorded by us.
  • Microphone. Only to speak in a live class. Audio is streamed to that class and is not recorded by us. Declining still lets you watch and listen.
  • Notifications. To send study reminders, class alerts and updates about your account. You can turn these off in the app or in Android settings.
  • Biometrics (fingerprint or face). If you enable biometric login, the check happens entirely on your device through Android's BiometricPrompt. Your fingerprint or face data never leaves your device and is never sent to us — we only store the fact that you switched the setting on.

Payment information

Purchases made inside the app are processed by Google Play Billing. If Google offers you an alternative payment option and you choose it, the payment is processed by Cashfree Payments. In both cases the payment is handled by that provider — we never receive or store your card, UPI or bank details. We store only what the purchase was, when it happened, the amount, and the provider's transaction reference, so we can unlock your content and handle refunds and support.

2. Why we use it

  • To create and secure your account, and to sign you in.
  • To deliver the study content, quizzes, classes and features you use.
  • To track your progress, streaks, coins and leaderboard position.
  • To process purchases and give you access to what you paid for.
  • To show ads, including personalised ads where permitted.
  • To send notifications you have not turned off.
  • To review reported content and enforce our community rules.
  • To detect fraud and abuse, and to meet our legal obligations.

3. Who we share it with

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with:

  • Google AdMob — to serve and measure ads.
  • Google Firebase — analytics, crash reporting and push notifications.
  • PostHog — product analytics.
  • Google Play Billing — in-app purchases.
  • Cashfree Payments — payments, where you choose that option.
  • Other users — your name, profile photo and public study stats are visible on leaderboards and in study rooms. Content you post in chat, peer reviews, or the marketplace is visible to other users by design.
  • Law enforcement or regulators — where we are legally required to, or to protect someone's safety.

4. Other users, reporting and blocking

Some parts of BPSCNotes let students see each other's content: study room chat, peer review of written answers, and the study material marketplace.

  • Reporting. Every such item has a report option. Reports go to our moderation team, which reviews them within 24 hours and removes content that breaks our rules.
  • Blocking. You can block any user from the same menu. You will stop seeing their messages, reviews and uploads. The person you block is not told. You can undo it at any time under Settings → Blocked users.

Content that breaks our rules — harassment, hate speech, sexual content, violence, spam, copyright infringement or sharing someone's personal information — is removed, and accounts that repeat it are suspended.

5. How long we keep it

  • Account and profile data: until you delete your account.
  • Study activity and progress: until you delete your account.
  • Purchase records: up to 8 years, as required by Indian tax law.
  • Moderation reports: up to 2 years, so repeat abuse can be identified.
  • Analytics: up to 14 months in aggregated form.

6. Deleting your account

You can delete your account and its data at any time, in two ways:

  • In the app: Settings → Delete Account. This takes effect immediately.
  • On the web: visit bpscnotes.in/account-deletion and submit a request, or email support@bpscnotes.in from your registered number's associated address.

Deleting your account removes your profile, study history, notes, coins and uploaded content. Purchase records are kept for the tax retention period above, and anonymised analytics that can no longer identify you may remain. Content you posted publicly may remain visible where removing it would break another user's records, but it is disassociated from your identity.

7. Your rights

You can ask us to show you the personal data we hold about you, correct anything wrong, delete your account, or stop sending you marketing messages. Email support@bpscnotes.in and we will respond within 30 days.

You can also turn off personalised ads on your device, revoke any permission in Android settings, and turn off notifications at any time.

8. Security

All traffic between the app and our servers uses HTTPS. Your session token is stored in Android's encrypted storage. Access to production data is limited to staff who need it. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your data and will tell you if a breach affects you.

9. Children

BPSCNotes is built for candidates preparing for the Bihar Public Service Commission examinations and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. If you believe a child has given us their information, contact us and we will delete it.

10. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top and, for significant changes, notify you in the app.
